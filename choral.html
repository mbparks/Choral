FIELD GUIDE / v1.0.0

Sound becomes structure.

A simpler creative path.

Use Sound → Habitat → Color → Grow → Save. Each step stays available; this is not a locked wizard. Easy controls cover everyday creation. Advanced reveals detailed audio, palette, lighting, ancestry, and cellular rules without resetting their values. On phones the controls form a compact bottom sheet; Hide controls exposes the whole scene.

Three ways to hear color.

Recorded keeps material history visible and suppresses live recoloring and emission pulses. New growth still records sound. Living temporarily shifts the hue of every visible material according to current sound while retaining its individual variation and saturation. Hybrid, the default, keeps the body history and allows localized young-tip, young-cell, and spore accents. Modes never rewrite recorded birth colors, mixed-palette appearance, ancestry, camera, or geometry.

Accent animation is separate: it controls localized highlights, not Living’s whole-body response. Recorded has no animated accents. Sound influence scales both new birth-color composition and live effects. With zero influence or a quiet/disconnected source, live recoloring recedes. Current scope applies to material restyling; Living is a temporary overlay even on preserved material.

Keep the color, reduce the movement.

Follow device respects the current reduced-motion preference, including changes during a session. Reduced disables orbit, live recoloring, accent animation, and interpolation. Entering reduced motion pauses growth; explicit Resume or Bloom can still advance it in discrete steps. It never resumes itself after a device setting changes back. Full motion is an explicit override. Still view pauses growth and selects Reduced without making the specimen monochrome. Palette and lighting changes remain available.

Interface themes change interface/background only; they do not change the sculpture palette, lighting, or halo strength. Import preserves this device’s current interface and motion preferences rather than imposing those from someone else’s specimen. The imported color-response and lighting choices remain portable.

C(H)ORAL combines recursive branching with a 32 × 32 × 32 cellular world. Branches seed living cells. Cell populations influence new branches. Sound changes the growth that follows, while older structure remains.

Connect something worth growing.

Share tab / device audio opens your browser’s sharing dialog. Choose the music-playing tab and enable its audio checkbox. Availability of whole-device or application audio depends on your browser and operating system; it is not guaranteed. The browser also requests a video track, but C(H)ORAL does not render, record, store, or transmit that track. All capture tracks stop when disconnected.

Open music file plays and analyzes a local file. Microphone listens without playing its input back through your speakers. Silent preview uses clearly labeled synthetic control signals, not audio. Play test sound generates a repeating test phrase and analyzes its real audio.

Listen to the shape.

Bass changes extension length and available growth. Midrange changes organic branching. High frequencies increase local mutation. Onsets can trigger new buds. RMS loudness controls the simulation’s pace. These are artistic mappings, not biological claims or music transcription.

Let every color grow.

Full Spectrum is the default for new configurations. Branches receive stable, varied hues, nearby cells form colorful spatial regions, and new growth has contrasting accents. Mature growth keeps its saturation. Colors coexist even when motion is off; this is not one hue cycling over the whole sculpture.

The library contains Full Spectrum, Reef, Aurora, Volcanic, Stained Glass, and Wild Type. Each has its own color distribution, branch contrast, accent, aging, variation, and mutation rules. Original Biolume, Aurora, Ember, and Monochrome remain under Legacy; the old Aurora is not silently replaced. New growth now records smoothed bass, midrange, high-frequency energy, loudness, and onsets together with its resulting base color. Bass favors structural color families; midrange shapes colonies; highs and onsets introduce contrasting accents. These artistic mappings depend on the palette. The neutral Monochrome palette deliberately remains neutral. Recorded and Hybrid preserve older body colors; Living offers an explicitly temporary whole-body recoloring layer. Branches now inherit their parent’s color identity. Cellular births inherit the locally dominant lineage from the previous generation; ties use a seeded spatial choice. Color mutation makes a persistent new hue identity, which can spread through descendants. Branches seed cell colors, and buds can inherit from cells. Sound still influences new colors, within the inheritance strength you choose.

Paint now, or grow into the next palette.

Restyle existing growth applies the selected palette and artist controls immediately to the visible specimen and future growth. The original birth records and ancestry are not overwritten. Affect new growth only preserves the current material treatment when selected. Later palette, diversity, saturation, and hue changes apply only to new births, leaving older branches and living cells in their retained treatment. Dying cells carry their retained look into spores. Return to Restyle existing growth to apply the current look to everything again. Neither mode resets geometry, moves the camera, or interrupts audio.

Color diversity opens or narrows the range of material tones. Hue bias rotates all material hues. Saturation adjusts their strength. The three layer hue controls act independently on skeleton, cells, and spores/halos. Reset color tuning restores those artist controls without changing the palette, ancestry, or scope. These controls are recorded in birth-time artist metadata and saved appearance. Glow and live highlights remain global lighting effects, not frozen material. For a static comparison, pause growth, disable orbit and live color accents, and keep lighting unchanged.

Palette previews show their unmodified default colors. Wild Type doubles the base mutation chance (capped at 100%) and increases within-family variation; Aurora reduces both. The interface shows the effective chance. Color mutation remains zero when its base control is zero. The lineage legend uses each identity’s most populous retained look when several palettes coexist; a chip is not the average color of the colony.

Color inheritance balances family resemblance against newly composed sound colors. Color mutation · base chance is the base probability of founding a contrasting variant when a birth has a known lineage. It does not change cell birth/death rules or branch geometry. Lineage variation, under Advanced, controls smaller differences within a family. Mature growth stays saturated, and spores carry their originating colors. The six legend chips show the largest cellular lineages; IDs remain stable even when their population ranking changes.

A separate seeded random stream drives color inheritance and mutation. Its state and pending offspring traits are included in specimen exports. Retired lineages are pruned when nothing refers to them; parent IDs may refer to retired ancestors, so this is not an unlimited genealogical archive.

Sound influence affects future birth colors and the strength of live accents; it does not rewrite saved birth records. Accent animation adds smoothly changing, localized highlights to tips, young cells, and selected spores. Turning it off leaves the underlying birth colors visible. Reduced motion also disables these effects and Living recoloring. Color smoothing controls how quickly newly recorded colors follow a new passage.

Play color study sends audible 90 Hz bass, 1,000 Hz midrange, and 6,500 Hz treble through the same analyser as your music. Cycle mode changes passage every five seconds. New seed clears the specimen; it does not restart playback. Silent preview and manual Bloom births are explicitly labeled as synthetic or manual in exported records, never as captured audio.

Light without losing the colors.

Sculpture is neutral studio lighting with underside fill. Bioluminescent emphasizes luminous cells and tips. Unlit Color shows unshaded material and disables surface light, self-emission, direction, and depth controls; halos remain optional. Selecting a treatment applies its starting light settings; subsequent changes show Custom. Reset this treatment restores only those lighting settings.

Surface illumination lights the material. Self-emission makes it luminous independently of those lights. Glow · halo intensity controls only the surrounding spore/cell halos, with bounded transparency instead of additive white glare. Turn off Spore halos to hide that layer. Interior / underside fill lifts visible recesses without revealing occluded geometry. Direction and depth adjust the studio light and subtle rear-material dimming. All these settings are global to the current view; they do not rewrite color history or restart audio.

The new renderer changes the lighting of older specimens, not their saved geometry, colors, or mixed-palette materials. Old Glow values are retained as halo intensity; new surface/emission controls receive safe defaults. v1.0 exports retain lighting, color response, and motion settings. Import honors the receiving device’s interface and motion preferences.

Keep what you grow.

Save PNG captures only the 3D view. Export specimen preserves the simulation and camera in JSON. Imports pause growth for inspection and disconnect audio; reconnect a source and resume when ready. Audio itself is never exported. Settings autosave locally, but the living specimen does not. C(H)ORAL v0.8, v0.7, v0.6, v0.5, v0.4, v0.3, v0.2 and original VIVARIUM v0.1 specimens are accepted. v0.4 ancestry and its original palette remain intact. v0.5 exports include mixed-palette appearances and per-birth artist controls; malformed appearance references are rejected before replacing the open specimen. v0.3 birth colors and sound history remain intact, but past ancestry is not invented. v0.2/v0.1 growth retains its earlier color formulas without fabricated sound history. New births establish color lineages when growth resumes. Exported feature summaries are not audio recordings and cannot reconstruct or replay the original sound. Settings are copied to a separate C(H)ORAL key when the old key is accessible on the same origin; the original key is left intact. A different website origin cannot read that key, so export/import is the portable route.

Preservation safeguards.

JSON export validates the portable snapshot before starting a download and includes a CRC-32 check for accidental corruption—not an authenticity signature. Confirm that the file actually downloaded. PNG is an image, not a resumable specimen. Imports validate geometry, cellular state, birth/color ancestry, materials, continuation state, settings, and camera before asking to replace the current sculpture. Cancel leaves audio and your specimen untouched. Habitat and seed changes warn about unexported work. Browser leave warnings are best-effort and cannot protect against crashes, forced closes, or every mobile lifecycle event.

The specimen badge is separate from settings autosave. Camera gestures, material edits, and further growth require another JSON export. Automatic orbit does not continually mark the specimen dirty. Graphics context recovery rebuilds the renderer in place; it never automatically reloads and discards the sculpture. JSON export remains available during a graphics interruption. Low quality samples fewer cells and halos but keeps stored geometry and color history; it also lowers the cap on future branch growth.

The edge of this habitat.

This is a bounded, experimental 3D automaton, not the classic 2D Conway rule and not a biological model. Mutations, branch seeding, and occasional low-population reseeding keep the artwork evolving. Cellular birth and survival are synchronous, with 26 neighbors and fixed empty boundaries. New births are restricted to a growth substrate deposited around branches, so each habitat retains its own spatial character. The branch cap pauses additional structural growth rather than silently deleting history. Rendering samples cells across the entire volume when the graphics budget is exceeded; every cell remains simulated.

Structural memory at 100 preserves every branch until reset or the cap. Lower values retire older branches by simulation age. The same seed reproduces initial conditions; live audio and timing can produce different futures. Recursion depth affects future growth, not existing branches.

Comfort and privacy.

No strobing or camera shake is used. Reduced motion stops auto orbit and live color/emission effects; Pause growth freezes the simulation. Accent animation is a separate localized-highlights control. Capture requires explicit permission. No uploads, analytics, remote fonts, or runtime network dependencies are used.
